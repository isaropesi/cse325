### 3. Brainstormed Project Ideas Under Consideration

#### Idea 1 (Primary): Personal Finance & Budget Tracker (Recommended)
*   **Description**: A comprehensive financial manager where users can securely register, log transactions, categorize spending, and monitor budget thresholds.
*   **Target Audience**: Students, young professionals, or anyone looking to track expenses and improve financial savings.
*   **Key Features**:
    *   User Authentication for secure, private accounts.
    *   CRUD operations for income and expenses (amount, date, description, category).
    *   Visual progress bars and charts indicating budget usage.
    *   Responsive design for mobile and desktop tracking.

#### Idea 2: Recipe & Meal Planner App
*   **Description**: A meal planning tool where users search, add, edit, and share recipes and generate shopping lists.
*   **Target Audience**: Home cooks, busy families, and meal preppers.
*   **Key Features**:
    *   CRUD for custom recipes and calendar plans.
    *   Authentication so users can manage their private recipe books and custom weekly menus.
    *   Automatic shopping list generation based on active meals.

#### Idea 3: Study Group Finder & Resource Sharing Platform
*   **Description**: A collaborative web app helping students find and join active study groups for their classes and share reference notes/materials.
*   **Target Audience**: University and high-school students.
*   **Key Features**:
    *   CRUD for study groups, session schedules, and note uploads.
    *   Auth to restrict access to verified students.
    *   Rating/comment system for shared study resources.
